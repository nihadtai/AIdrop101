# Stage One: Product to Store

Research produces candidates and can request deeper market research. Sourcing compares CJ/AliExpress/1688 paths and unit economics. Supplier approval can use manual links, a backup source, or 1688 image search; it creates a product-supplier binding before storefront work.

Store configuration allows automatic or manual design choices. `agents/store_builder.py` creates the store/copy artifacts; store and stage-one approval decide whether to archive the candidate and restart research or advance to creative work.

Related files: `agents/product_research.py`, `agents/market_research.py`, `agents/sourcing.py`, `agents/store_builder.py`, `tools/binding_store.py`.
